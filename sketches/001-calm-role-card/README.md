## Variant: Calm role card

### Design stance
A reassuring, editorial split layout makes the university feel like a trusted service rather than a complex administration system.

### Key choices
- Layout: Spacious two-panel composition with a focused card.
- Typography: Large welcoming headline, restrained utility text.
- Color: Soft sage background with deep teal action color.
- Interaction: Role buttons update the fictional identity list and primary action; entry produces a visible confirmation.

### Trade-offs
- Strong at: Trust, clarity, first-time orientation, responsive adaptation.
- Weak at: Fast switching for power users who already know their account.

### Best for
- Student-facing portals and institutional homepages where reassurance matters.
