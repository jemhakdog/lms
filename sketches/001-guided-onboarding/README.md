## Variant: Guided onboarding

### Design stance
A warm, step-based welcome asks one simple question first, reducing the cognitive load of choosing among four portal audiences.

### Key choices
- Layout: Centered single-column onboarding flow.
- Typography: Editorial serif display paired with plain system UI text.
- Color: Warm paper background, terracotta accent, charcoal action.
- Interaction: Role tiles select a path; Continue reveals the next step without leaving the sketch.

### Trade-offs
- Strong at: Friendly orientation, student confidence, clear progressive disclosure.
- Weak at: Adds a step for returning users and gives less immediate account visibility.

### Best for
- First-time visitors and universities that want role selection to feel welcoming rather than administrative.
