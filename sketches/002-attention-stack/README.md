## Variant: Attention stack

### Design stance
The dashboard exists to remove noise. A single prioritized stack answers "what should I do next" before anything else.

### Key choices
- Layout: Centered editorial column, single stack of three cards, summary band above.
- Typography: Serif Iowan/Palatino display for warmth; sans-serif for utility text and chips.
- Color: Soft sage background, deep teal accent, terracotta for urgency, mossy green for confirmations.
- Interaction: One primary CTA per card (Open queue / Reply / Confirm). After-the-stack chips for less-urgent lookups.

### Trade-offs
- Strong at: Reducing decision fatigue, building trust, supporting interrupted sessions, mobile.
- Weak at: Power users who want to see everything at once; the schedule disappears from view.

### Best for
- Faculty who feel overwhelmed by dashboards full of widgets and want a clear "do this next" surface, especially mid-semester.
