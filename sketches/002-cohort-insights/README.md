## Variant: Cohort insights

### Design stance
Faculty work is teaching *people*, not pushing tasks. The dashboard makes students the hero and surfaces who's thriving, who's at risk, and where to spend attention — schedule is supporting context.

### Key choices
- Layout: Two-column. Left: course snapshot + today's day. Right: student roster with engagement flags, sorted by who needs attention.
- Typography: Serif Iowan/Palatino for narrative display (headlines, course names); sans-serif for data and chips.
- Color: Warm paper background, terracotta accent, teal/gold/rose as a three-state status palette (thriving / watch / at risk).
- Interaction: Course cards selectable (one shown active); segmented control to filter roster; legend explains flag colors.

### Trade-offs
- Strong at: Instructor-as-mentor framing, surfacing at-risk students, narrative reporting, warm institutional feel.
- Weak at: Operational efficiency (grading queue, schedule timing) is secondary; harder to act on individual items.

### Best for
- Faculty who see themselves primarily as educators of people, in institutions that value relationship and student success as the brand promise.
